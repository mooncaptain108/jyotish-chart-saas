# Jyotish Query
### Interface Tweaks

Don't like how something looks? Small text, faint lines or the colours hard to read on your screen? Settings &gt; **Interface Tweaks** changes the look of the charts and panels without changing any calculation. Every switch takes effect immediately, so you can try one and switch it back. Your choices are saved in this browser (or in the app) and stay set the next time you open Jyotish Query.

**Degrees**
- **4:30 / 4.50** &mdash; show degrees as degrees:minutes or as decimals.
- **Degrees bold** &mdash; bold degree labels on the charts.

**Planets**
- **Su Mo / glyphs** &mdash; show planets as two-letter names or as astrological symbols. The Analysis popover follows the same choice.
- **Bold**, **White** &mdash; make the planet names or symbols on the charts bolder or white.

**Signs**
- **Ar 1 / glyphs** &mdash; show signs as abbreviations and numbers or as zodiac symbols.
- **Bold**, **White** &mdash; make the house sign numbers or symbols bolder or white.

**Menus &amp; panels**
- **Menu labels bold** &mdash; bolder toolbar buttons and menu items.
- **Panel labels white**, **Panel labels bold** &mdash; the birth details above the chart, the divisional chart list, the Rashi Data and Dasha Periods titles, and the Dasha Now/Top buttons.
- **Dasha Periods dates white, planets bold** &mdash; easier-to-read Dasha dates.
- **Dasha Periods slash white** &mdash; the slash between Maha and Antar dasha planets.
- **Rashi Data text white** &mdash; the Sunrise line and the degree, sign and Nakshatra columns.
- **Transit View text: remove all spaces** &mdash; squeezes the planet text in the Transits view so it fits small houses.

**Dialogs**
- **Analysis bold** &mdash; every line of the Analysis popover in bold.
- **Analysis text plain** &mdash; the Analysis and Muhurta Eval text in plain white instead of gold and tan. Many people find this easier to read.

**Chart lines**
- **Chart lines white**, **Chart lines bold** &mdash; brighter or heavier lines on the main chart and the divisional chart.

The light or dark theme itself is the **Lite** / **Dark** button on the toolbar.
