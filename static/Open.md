# Welcome.
Thanks for visiting **Jyotish Query**. The primary purpose of JQ is to search for Muhurta **CANDIDATES**. It can also render charts given location and birth times.

All your data is stored on your system. No data is stored on the server that is running Jyotish Query.
Please review the Data Storage explanation in Help/Data Storage.

<u>**To get started you will need to enter in at least one location in the Atlas.**</u>

Click the Gear icon to see more options.
